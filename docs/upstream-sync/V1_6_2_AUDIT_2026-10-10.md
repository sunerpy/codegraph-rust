## 5. Graph and version facts

- The program moves the extraction version once, 21 → 22, and the schema
  8 → 9 in the same commit (W1-00). An index built by `v0.54.0` reads as
  outdated and is rebuilt once by `codegraph sync`, a daemon's catch-up or the
  first MCP access; `v0.54.0` refuses an index built by `v0.55.0`.
- The node-id formula does not change (AGENTS.md invariant 3). Ids that move
  because their data moves (OD-5) are listed in
  [`equivalence.md`](../equivalence.md).
- Every golden change is regenerated by `scripts/regen-goldens.sh` and
  recorded in the regeneration log of [`equivalence.md`](../equivalence.md).
